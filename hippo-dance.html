The Dance of Hippocleides

GREEK 1010

The tyrant Cleisthenes organizes a pan-Hellenic contest to select the husband of his daughter. After one year of testing the suitors (μνηστῆρος), Cleisthenes calls all the candidates together for a feast to announce the winner. Although Hippocleides leads in the competition and is due to be named the victor, he makes a grave mistake.
