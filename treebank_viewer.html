About Treebank Viewer

The Treebank Viewer is an interactive tool designed to read and explore linguistically annotated texts formatted in PROIEL XML.

  • Interactive Text: Click any word in the reader to reveal its underlying dictionary form (lemma), part-of-speech, and morphology.
  • Dependency Trees: Selecting a word instantly generates its sentence's syntactic dependency tree in the right-hand panel.
  • Conjunction Highlighting: Nested conjunction phrases (headed by a C- token) are automatically detected and visualized using multi-colored underlines.

Need Data?

You can download PROIEL XML treebank data directly from the Syntacticus treebank data repository.
