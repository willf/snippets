Mathematical Logic

1. Age Calculation (A)

A = Ycurr − Ybirth

2. Age-Based Decade (D)

Dage = A − ( A mod 10 )

3. Historical Era (E)

Ehist = Ycurr − ( Ycurr mod 10 )

Where nmodd is the remainder after division. For positive integers, subtracting the remainder is equivalent to the floor-division method.
